ABOUT ADVENTURE ESCAPE SA

Adventure Escape SA was established in 2024 by Liam Daniels. We provide professionally guided outdoor adventure experiences throughout the Western Cape.

Our activities are designed for individuals, families, tourists, schools and corporate groups. We aim to make outdoor adventures enjoyable, exciting and accessible to everyone.

OUR GOAL

Explore Nature and Adventure

Our goal is to provide safe, exciting and enjoyable outdoor experiences while promoting nature, teamwork and eco-tourism. We want every customer to leave with unforgettable memories.

WHO WE SERVE

Families

Enjoy outdoor experiences and create special memories together.

Tourists

Discover the natural beauty of the Western Cape through adventure.

Schools

Encourage learning, confidence and teamwork through outdoor activities.

Corporate Groups

Build stronger teams through exciting group activities.
