ABOUT US

Adventure Escape SA was established in 2024 by Liam Daniels. We provide professionally guided outdoor adventure experiences throughout the Western Cape.

Our activities are designed for individuals, families, tourists, schools and corporate groups.

OUR GOAL

Our goal is to provide safe, exciting and enjoyable outdoor experiences while promoting nature and eco-tourism.
